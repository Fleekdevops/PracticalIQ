How to Summarize and Analyze Long PDFs with Adobe Acrobat AI Assistant

Instantly extract key deliverables, financial metrics, and hidden clauses from a massive contract, PDF, or market research report using Adobe’s AI Assistant.

4 stepsCopy-and-paste prompt included
  1. 01

    Open your document

    Launch Adobe Acrobat (or open it online) and upload the PDF you need to review, such as a regional market analysis or a local supply contract.

  2. 02

    Activate the AI Assistant

    Click the AI Assistant icon in the top right corner of the toolbar. The AI will immediately begin processing the document.

  3. 03

    Get an instant summary

    Before you type a word, the AI Assistant generates a high-level overview of the document, plus suggested quick-click questions.

  4. 04

    Use a precision prompt

    To get deep, actionable answers instead of generic summaries, copy and paste this workflow prompt into the AI Assistant chat box.

    Prompt
    Analyze this document and provide a bulleted summary tailored for a business owner. Specifically highlight:
1. The core deliverables or expectations from our side.
2. The exact financial obligations, payment timelines, and milestones.
3. Any potential risks, penalty clauses, or termination conditions.
Structure the response cleanly with bold headers for quick reading.

AI summaries can miss details. For contracts, have the final terms checked by a qualified person before you sign.

Smart work tip

Stop wasting hours on fine print

Let AI do the heavy lifting so you can focus on execution.
